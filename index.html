Markdown family

This kit belongs between profiles, noticeboards and grants.

The asset files should not replace the public profile or the private aura layer. They give media crews, event helpers, grant writers and local organisers a clearer view of practical capacity.

The working boundary is simple: publish public capability and approved needs; keep private addresses, expensive gear locations, login details, cultural permissions, raw contacts and safety-sensitive notes out of public files.
