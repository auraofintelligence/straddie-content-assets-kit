Boundary

Public capability is not public consent.

An asset list can say a team has a microphone, projector, camera or editing workflow. It should not imply permission to film people, share cultural material, use private locations, publish names, or expose storage and login details.

When in doubt, keep the generated Markdown as a private working file until a human has reviewed the public-safe layer.
