# Shared Assets

## Shared Purpose

Make small local content jobs possible without every person needing to own every tool.

## Shared Gear List

- Wireless mic kit | steward needed | interviews and public updates
- Tripod and phone clamp | easy share | starter filming and live notes
- Projector and screen | event approval needed | film nights and community briefings

## Borrowing or Check-Out Rules

- One steward approves the loan.
- Gear is checked before and after use.
- Batteries, cables and storage cards are logged.
- Public events need weather, power and trip-hazard checks.

## Private or Permission-Needed Assets

- Expensive gear storage locations
- Private contact details
- Cultural or protected place access notes
- Login details and paid accounts
