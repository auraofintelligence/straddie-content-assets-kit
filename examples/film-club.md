# Film Club

This is a starter planning file inside the Straddie Content Assets Kit. It can become the seed brief for a future standalone film club repo, but that repo has not been created yet.

## Core Purpose

Help locals move from scattered phone clips and event ideas into shared screenings, practical skills, local story development and small finished films.

## People and Roles

- Coordinator | keeps dates, files and questions together
- Screening helper | venue, projector, sound and pack-down
- Story helper | interviews, release notes and local context
- Asset steward | shared gear, batteries, storage and check-out rules
