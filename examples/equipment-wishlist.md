# Equipment Wishlist

## Requested Items

- Wireless lapel mic pair | clearer speech outdoors | $250-$450 | high
- Phone cage or grip | steadier handheld clips | $80-$180 | medium
- Portable LED light | indoor interviews and evening setup | $120-$300 | medium
- Two labelled SSDs | safer file handover and backups | $220-$420 | high

## Borrow, Hire or In-Kind Options

- Local venue may store projection gear for event day.
- Sponsor could fund microphones in exchange for a public-safe thank-you.
- Existing laptop can stay in use until the workflow proves itself.

## Do Not Buy Yet

- Cinema camera body before audio, storage and team habits are stable
- Drone before permissions, insurance and pilot capability are clear
